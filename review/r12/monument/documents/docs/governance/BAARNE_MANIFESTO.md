# BAARNE Manifesto V0.1

2026-10-01 · CURRENT DESIGN COMMITMENT · Our principles, not legal terms or a claim that every planned service exists. See [implemented controls and limits](POLICY_TRACEABILITY.md).

**BAARNE builds networks.** BAARNE does not create the value of the network. Its members do. BAARNE creates the conditions for them to do so.

**Trust belongs to the network, not to BAARNE.** No member can buy trust. Trust can only be earned. BAARNE administrators may operate BAARNE services, but they may not rewrite independently established trust history.

**Every member’s safety has equal worth.** No member’s safety is worth more than another member’s. Money, wealth, subscription tier or commercial status must never determine the priority of a safety or help request. Any future safety service must follow this principle; M0 has no safety-request service.

**We collect what we need, not what we can.** Private lives do not belong on public blockchains. People should understand what becomes public before they choose to publish it.

**Decentralize what must be trusted. Centralize what must be operated.** BAARNE should never require a member to trust BAARNE where independent verification is reasonably possible.

**BAARNE is decentralized by design, but blockchain-independent by architecture.** No BAARNE identity or trust relationship should permanently depend on the continued operation of a specific blockchain, RPC provider or infrastructure vendor. Today’s evidence adapter is one implementation, not the meaning of trust.

**Blockchain is evidence infrastructure, not the product.** BAARNE should not require members to understand cryptocurrency in order to participate. The current developer tooling has not yet achieved that member experience.

These principles guide decisions. Where a control is missing, we will say so. Independent verification can establish who authorized a record and what was published; it cannot decide whether a person deserves trust.
